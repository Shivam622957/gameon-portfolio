If you've ever tried to book a cricket ground or find a badminton court in your city on a weekend, you know the problem. You don't know which grounds are near you. The ones you do know are full, closed, or impossible to reach.

India has more than 100 million recreational athletes who want to play more often. The friction sits between them and the field. GameOn's job is to remove that friction.

We pay the highest commission share in India to our venue partners — 95–97% of every booking — because the people who own the field deserve more than the platform that lists it.

Business Plan

How GameOn
Makes Money

Multiple revenue streams from day one — with tournaments scaling into our dominant income engine at 20–30 grounds.

💳 Primary · Active at Launch

Booking Platform Commission

GameOn charges a 3–5% platform fee on every booking made through our app. The venue keeps 95–97%. With 20 venues at launch each averaging 8–10 bookings a day, this generates meaningful revenue from week one — and scales linearly with every new ground added.

₹500–₹3K Avg booking value
3–5% Platform fee
🏆 Secondary · Scales at 20–30 Grounds

Tournament Revenue

When we reach 20–30 ground partners, we automate the full tournament workflow — scheduling, draws, scoring, results — and run 2–4 tournaments per month across academy kids, corporate teams, and competitive players. We retain 20–30% of total entry pool.

Tournament Engine

Tournaments: Our Long Game

When we have 20–30 grounds on board, we automate the entire tournament workflow. This is where GameOn becomes the operating system for recreational sports competition in India — four distinct audiences, all underserved.

🎓

Academy Kids

Structured competitions for kids in sports academies — cricket, football, badminton. Regular, recurring, year-round. Parents pay willingly for structured competition that complements training.

💼

Corporate Professionals

Competitive and fun leagues for working professionals. Corporate vs corporate, inter-department formats. High willingness to pay, strong brand sponsorship potential, annual recurring.

⚡

Competitive Players

Players who want to compete as much as possible — weekly or bi-weekly formats with rankings, leaderboards, and pathways to bigger events. Our most loyal, highest-engagement users.

🎉

Corporate Fun Events

Team-building formats, mixed-sport days, "office olympics." High spend per head, high frequency. Sold as complete packages including venue, logistics, and post-event celebration.

01

Untapped, Fragmented Market

India has 100M+ recreational athletes and almost no organised booking infrastructure. The market runs on WhatsApp groups and phone calls. GameOn is the first-mover building a tech-first, nationwide solution.

02

High Partner Loyalty — 95–97% Payout

At 95–97% of every booking, venue partners have almost no reason to leave. Each ground onboarded is a sticky, long-term asset. Our model aligns incentives: we win only when they win.

03

Tournaments = Scalable Revenue Engine

Tournament revenue is high-margin, recurring, and grows with the ground network. At 20–30 grounds we automate the entire workflow and launch structured leagues — creating a self-reinforcing flywheel.

04

Network Effects + Supply Moat

More grounds → more tournaments → more players → more bookings → more revenue for owners → more grounds join. The school grounds program is a supply moat that would take years to replicate.

05

Right Timing — India's Sports Boom

Sports participation is at an all-time high. UPI penetration exceeds 90% in urban India. India's youth cohort (35% under 20) is the largest-ever sports consumer base. The infrastructure needs to catch up — right now.

Our DNA

Three principles drive
how we build.

01

Players First.

Every product decision passes a simple test: does it help a player play sooner, more often, or with better company? If yes, we ship it. If no, we don't.

02

Partners, not inventory.

We don't squeeze small business owners. We share 95–97% of every booking back to the venue — the highest payout in India. No hidden fees or exclusivity locks.

03

India-first, not imitating.

Built for how Indians actually play — with UPI, in Hinglish, on patchy 4G. We don't paste a US sports app over India and hope it works.
