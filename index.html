Making Sports

🏛️   CIN: U93290UW2026PTC252581  ·  Ghaziabad, UP

If you've ever tried to book a cricket ground or find a badminton court in your city on a weekend, you know the problem. You don't know which grounds are near you. The ones you do know are full, closed, or impossible to reach.

India has more than 100 million recreational athletes who want to play more often. The friction sits between them and the field. GameOn's job is to remove that friction.

We pay the highest commission share in India to our venue partners — 95–97% of every booking — because the people who own the field deserve more than the platform that lists it.

"It shouldn't be that hard to find a place to play."
